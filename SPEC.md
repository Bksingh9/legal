# LegalDesk AI — Product Specification (v1.0)

## 0. North Star
A non-engineer-buildable, BCI-compliant legal-help platform for India that monetizes
through three layered tiers: AI triage (free/freemium) → document automation (₹199–₹1,499)
→ matched lawyer consultation (₹9–₹15/min). Built solo with Claude Code, deployed on
Vercel + Supabase, with WhatsApp as the primary user channel.

Target: ₹27L MRR by month 12, ₹2.1Cr MRR by month 24.

## 1. Strict Non-Negotiables (Compliance)
- Bar Council of India Rule 36 prohibits advocate advertising. Therefore:
  - No public lawyer profiles, names, photos, or testimonials.
  - All lawyer-side branding is anonymized (specialization + years + rating only).
  - Position the platform as "client query intake + document automation",
    not "browse and book a named lawyer".
  - Disclaimer on every legal output: "Generated by LegalDesk AI. Not legal advice.
    Consult a qualified advocate for case-specific opinion."
- DPDP Act 2023 compliance: explicit consent for PII, deletion on request,
  data residency in India (Supabase ap-south-1).
- All consultation calls recorded only with both-party consent prompt.

## 2. User Personas
P1. Urban salaried Indian (25–45) facing a one-off legal issue (notice, tenant, consumer).
P2. SMB owner needing recurring document and notice support.
P3. NRI handling Indian property, family, or inheritance matters.
P4. Tier-2/3 city user who needs vernacular (Hindi first, then Tamil/Telugu/Bengali/Marathi)
    voice-first triage.

## 3. Product Surface

### Tier 1 — AI Triage (free, email-gated)
- Chat input (text + voice) in Hindi/English.
- Claude classifies into: criminal | civil | family | property | consumer | labour |
  corporate | tax | cyber | other.
- Output: 1-page Case Prep PDF with (a) plain-language summary, (b) likely legal
  framework/sections, (c) suggested next 3 steps, (d) documents to gather, (e) urgency level.
- Soft CTA: "Generate the legal document" (Tier 2) OR "Get a 15-min lawyer call" (Tier 3).

### Tier 2 — Document Automation (₹199–₹1,499 one-time)
Launch SKUs:
- Legal notice (any cause): ₹499
- Reply to legal notice: ₹699
- Rent agreement (11-month): ₹399
- Consumer complaint (NCDRC format): ₹599
- RTI application: ₹199
- Employment NDA / offer letter: ₹599
- Cheque bounce notice (S.138 NI Act): ₹699
- Add-on: Lawyer review (24h SLA): +₹499

Flow: User answers a guided form → Claude fills template → preview → pay → PDF + DOC
delivered to email + WhatsApp.

### Tier 3 — Talk to a Lawyer (₹9–₹15/min)
- 15-min pack ₹199, 30-min pack ₹349, 60-min pack ₹599 (intro pricing).
- Match in <4 hours by specialization + language + state.
- Razorpay holds funds; Exotel masked-number callback OR 100ms video room.
- Auto-transcript (Claude) + summary email post-call.
- Lawyer earns 55–65% net of platform fee + GST.
- "Not satisfied" → free re-match or 50% wallet credit.

### Tier 4 — Subscription "LegalDesk Plus" (₹999/year)
- Unlimited document generation
- 60 mins/year of consultation included
- Priority callback (<1 hour)
- Document vault + reminders for renewals (rent agreements etc.)

### Tier 5 — B2B "LegalDesk for Business" (₹4,999–₹19,999/month)
- HR teams, real estate brokers, fintech partners.
- API access to document automation.
- Bulk legal notice issuance for collections.
- Dedicated lawyer pool.

## 4. Tech Stack (Locked)
- Next.js 14 App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind + shadcn/ui
- Supabase (Postgres + Auth + Storage + RLS) on ap-south-1
- Anthropic Claude API (claude-sonnet-4 default; haiku for cheap classification)
- Razorpay (payments + Route for lawyer payouts) + Stripe (NRI)
- Exotel (number masking) + 100ms (video)
- WhatsApp via AiSensy or Gupshup
- Resend (email), PostHog (analytics), Sentry (errors)
- Vercel (hosting), GitHub (source), Cloudflare (DNS + WAF)

## 5. Database Schema (initial)
- users(id, phone, email, name, locale, created_at, kyc_status, role[user|lawyer|admin])
- lawyers(id, user_id, bar_council_id, state, specializations[], years_exp, rating,
  payout_account, status[pending|verified|suspended])
- queries(id, user_id, raw_text, classification, urgency, prep_pdf_url, created_at)
- documents(id, user_id, type, status, input_json, output_pdf_url, paid, lawyer_review_id)
- consultations(id, user_id, lawyer_id, type[call|video], scheduled_at, duration_sec,
  recording_url, transcript_url, summary, amount_inr, lawyer_payout_inr, status)
- payments(id, user_id, razorpay_payment_id, amount, status, sku, idempotency_key)
- subscriptions(id, user_id, plan, status, started_at, renews_at)
- wallet(user_id, balance_inr, ledger[])
- audit_log(actor, action, target, payload, created_at)

All tables have RLS. No row leaves the org without an authenticated user_id match.

## 6. Acquisition Strategy (BCI-safe)
- SEO: 200 informational articles (e.g. "How to file a consumer complaint in India").
  Generated by Claude, reviewed by founder, never named lawyer endorsements.
- Reddit organic: r/LegalAdviceIndia, r/India, r/personalfinanceindia, r/IndianTeenagers
  (parents asking on behalf of family).
- WhatsApp viral: every doc generated has "Made with LegalDesk AI — try free triage" footer.
- NRI Facebook groups, Quora answers, YouTube Shorts on legal procedures.
- Partnerships: PropTech (rent agreements), HRtech (offer letters), fintech (S.138 notices).
- Referral: ₹100 wallet credit per signed-up friend, ₹250 on first paid txn.

## 7. KPIs
- Activation: % of signups that complete a triage within 24h. Target 50%.
- Conversion: % of triages that purchase Tier 2 within 7 days. Target 8%.
- Tier 3 attach: % of Tier 2 buyers who book a call within 30 days. Target 20%.
- Subscription conversion: % of paying users on Plus. Target 15%.
- Retention: M3 cohort revenue retention. Target 40%.
- Lawyer fill rate: % of bookings matched <4h. Target 90%.
- Refund/dispute rate. Target <3%.

## 8. Risk Register
- BCI/State Bar disciplinary action against onboarded lawyers → mitigate with anonymized
  profiles and platform-only branding.
- Razorpay account hold for "legal services" category → keep MCC clean, document compliance.
- AI hallucination producing incorrect legal advice → "lawyer review" gate for any
  output that goes into a court/tribunal; loud disclaimer everywhere.
- Lawyer no-show on call → 100ms penalty deduction + auto-rematch.
- Supply collapse if early lawyers don't earn → cap Tier 3 demand to active lawyers,
  use AI to convert overflow into Tier 2 doc sales.

## 9. Build Order (immutable for first 90 days)
- Week 1–2: scaffold + landing + waitlist
- Week 3–4: Tier 1 AI triage
- Week 5–6: Tier 2 document automation (5 SKUs) + Razorpay
- Week 7–8: lawyer onboarding + KYC backend
- Week 9–10: Tier 3 consultation flow (Exotel + 100ms)
- Week 11–12: subscriptions + referral + 50 SEO articles + bug bash
